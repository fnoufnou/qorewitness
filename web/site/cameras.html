Cameras

Does your camera work with QoreWitness?

If your camera can share a video stream on your home network over RTSP or ONVIF, QoreWitness can very likely seal it. Here is what we know, brand by brand, and how to check your own camera in two minutes.

How to read this list

Honest statuses, nothing more.

We only call a camera tested when we have run it with QoreWitness ourselves. Everything else is based on what the camera makers document, and we have no partnership with any of them.

  • Tested by us

    We run this camera with QoreWitness ourselves.

  • Works with RTSP

    The maker documents a standard RTSP stream, which is what QoreWitness records. We have not tested these models ourselves.

  • Not supported

    The camera offers no stream on your home network, so QoreWitness cannot record it.

Works with RTSP

These brands document a standard RTSP stream for their wired and PoE cameras. The setup guide has short hints for each one.

BrandStatusHow to switch on the stream
TP-Link Tapo (wired models)Works with RTSPCamera Account in the Tapo app. Battery models are not supported.
Reolink (wired and PoE)Works with RTSPSwitch on RTSP in the network settings of the app or client.
Hikvision and AnnkeWorks with RTSPUsually on already. ONVIF under Integration Protocol.
Dahua and AmcrestWorks with RTSPUsually on already. ONVIF in the network settings.
Ubiquiti UniFi ProtectWorks with RTSPSwitch on an RTSP stream in the camera settings and copy the link.
AxisWorks with RTSPOn as standard. Create a user for QoreWitness.
Other ONVIF camerasWorks with RTSPSwitch on ONVIF and create an ONVIF user.

Not supported

Battery cameras sleep to save power, and cloud-only cameras send their video straight to the maker's servers. Neither offers a stream on your home network, so QoreWitness has nothing to record.

CameraStatusWhy
Most Ring cameras and doorbellsNot supportedCloud only, no local stream.
ArloNot supportedBattery or cloud based on most models.
BlinkNot supportedBattery and cloud only.
Google Nest camerasNot supportedCloud only, no local stream.
Tapo battery cameras and battery doorbellsNot supportedNo stream while on battery. Some wired doorbells are the exception.

Try it yourself

How to check your camera in 2 minutes

The free VLC player opens the same kind of stream that QoreWitness records. If VLC shows your camera's picture, QoreWitness will very likely work with it too.

  1. Find your camera's address in the camera app or in your router, for example 192.168.1.20.
  2. Switch on the camera's stream and note its account name and password. The brand hints show where.
  3. Install VLC on your computer and open it.
  4. Choose Media, then Open Network Stream (on a Mac: File, then Open Network).
  5. Type the stream address and press Play.

For a Tapo camera, the address looks like this:

rtsp://NAME:PASSWORD@192.168.1.20:554/stream1

Do you see your camera's picture? Then you are ready to set it up.

No picture?
  • Check the address, the account name and the password. Letters and digits are the safest choice for both.
  • Check the stream path for your brand in the brand hints. Searching for your model with the words "RTSP URL" usually finds it.
  • Close the live view in the camera app, because some cameras allow only a few viewers at once.

Camera ready?

Install QoreWitness and finish the setup in your browser.
