      What this proves

      The file is exactly the one the camera's QoreWitness recorded, down to the last byte. It existed at the time shown, because its fingerprint was written to a public ledger, the QoreChain test network, and signed by the camera.

      What it cannot prove

      It does not prove who is in the video or that the scene was real. It does not prove who owns the camera. The camera clock sets the recording time, and the seal time comes from the public record. QoreWitness is in beta on the QoreChain test network, which can be reset, and a seal is not a legal timestamp.

      Technical details

      Checked against the QoreChain test network. A record on a public ledger is not an eIDAS qualified timestamp. It proves that the file existed at a time. It does not prove who recorded it or that the scene was real.
