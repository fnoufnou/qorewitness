QoreWitness

A post-quantum evidence anchor for camera footage on QoreChain.

QoreWitness hashes every video segment from a home camera. Every 10 minutes it builds a Merkle root of the hashes, signs the root with ML-DSA-87 and stores it on QoreChain. The contract checks the post-quantum signature on chain. Later, anyone can prove that a clip existed before a known time and was not changed.

Try it yourself

  1. Download the demo clip and its receipt.
  2. Open the verifier. It runs in your browser. The clip does not leave your computer.
  3. Drop both files on the verifier. It checks the hash, the Merkle path, the camera key, the ML-DSA-87 signature and the record on chain.
  4. Change one byte of the clip, or trim it, and verify again. The check fails.

Limits

  • Proof of concept for Build Before Q-Day, the QoreChain Developer Fest. Testnet only.
  • An anchor shows that a file existed before a time. It does not show that the scene in front of the camera was real.
  • A blockchain anchor is not an eIDAS qualified timestamp.
