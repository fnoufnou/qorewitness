Why it matters

AI made every video easy to doubt.

Free AI tools can change a face, a licence plate or a time stamp in minutes. The result can look as real as the original. Judges, insurers and investigators know this. So they ask one hard question about every clip: can you prove that nobody changed it afterwards?

Most camera owners cannot. A recording sits on a hard disk or in a cloud account for days or weeks before anyone needs it. During that time, anyone with access could edit it. A defence lawyer only has to raise that doubt to weaken strong footage.

QoreWitness gives each recording a digital fingerprint within minutes of capture. It signs that fingerprint and writes it to a public blockchain. Change one pixel later, and the fingerprint no longer matches.

You can then prove that a clip existed before anyone knew about the incident. That removes the main reason to doubt it, and your cameras become reliable witnesses again.

Proof of time

Proof of integrity

A single changed byte breaks the match. The verifier shows the failed check at once.

Proof that lasts

Evidence can matter for 20 years. QoreWitness uses signatures designed to resist future quantum computers.

How it works

Sealed at the source. Checked by anyone.

Your footage stays on your own recorder. Only fingerprints go to the chain.

  1. 01

    Fingerprint

    The recorder gives every new video segment a SHA-256 fingerprint.

  2. 02

    Bundle

    Every 10 minutes, QoreWitness combines the fingerprints into one Merkle root.

  3. 03

    Sign

    The camera's own key signs the root with ML-DSA-87, a post-quantum signature.

  4. 04

    Anchor

    QoreChain stores the root. Its smart contract checks the signature before it accepts the record.

To verify, you drop a clip and its receipt into the browser verifier. It repeats every check against the public chain. You need no account and no QoreWitness server.

Principles

Designed for evidence that must hold up.

  • Private by design

    Only fingerprints leave your network. Nobody can rebuild a video from a fingerprint.

  • Quantum-safe

    QoreWitness signs with ML-DSA-87 from NIST FIPS 204. The contract checks each signature on chain.

  • Open and verifiable

    The code is open source. The verifier is one HTML file with a published SHA-256, so you can rebuild it and compare.

Status

Early alpha, running today.

QoreWitness is in early alpha. It runs as a working pilot on the QoreChain testnet. Since 9 November 2026, a home camera has signed and anchored its footage every 10 minutes, around the clock.

Next on the roadmap: a second, independent timestamp on Bitcoin through OpenTimestamps, and a guided installer for common camera systems.

Alpha · testnet

Know the limits

  • Testnet only. A network reset can remove testnet records.
  • A record proves that a file existed at a time. It cannot prove that the scene in front of the camera was real.
  • A blockchain record is not an eIDAS qualified timestamp. A court weighs it as ordinary evidence.
