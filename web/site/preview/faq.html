FAQ

Questions people ask.

Short, honest answers. If your question is not here, ask on X or in the QoreChain Discord.

Does my video leave my home?

No. Your video stays on your box at home. Only fingerprints and times go on the public network. The network can also see the account address of your box and when each seal was made.

A fingerprint shows nothing about what is in the picture or the sound.

What if my internet is down?

Recording goes on. Sealing needs the internet, so the minutes without a connection probably have no seal. The network shows the gap with its start and length.

Nobody can fill the gap later with a backdated seal.

Is this crypto?

It uses a public blockchain as a notary. You do not need to buy or trade anything to look at proof. On Fees included you never touch tokens.

During the pilot, all tokens are test tokens with no value.

What does it cost?

Free during the testnet pilot, for both plans. No payment is collected.

Later, Self-managed stays free software and you pay the network fees yourself. Fees included is planned at about EUR 4 per camera per month (estimate). See Plans.

Will the police or a court accept it?

We cannot promise that. A court can accept a QoreWitness record, but it weighs it as ordinary evidence. We are not an EU qualified time stamp provider.

Pilot records show how the method works. They are not evidence yet. Ask a lawyer about your case.

Which cameras work?

Cameras that give a network video stream (RTSP). One camera runs live today. We are testing the TP-Link Tapo C210 now.

We plan to publish a tested list and to grow it. We have no partnership with any camera maker.

What if I lose the box?

The seals on the network stay. To prove a clip you also need the clip and its receipt. So back them up.

A one-click export of clips and receipts as one ZIP is coming in the pilot. It is not built yet. Until then, copy the clip by hand and download its receipt from the dashboard. See Daily use.

Can QoreWitness show that the scene was real?

No. It shows that the file did not change after it was sealed. A staged scene or a fake stream is sealed the same way as a real one.

Can someone fake a clip from inside my home network?

It is hard but not impossible. Someone who controls your box or your camera before sealing could feed it fake footage.

Keep the box and the camera on their own network. Use strong passwords. Read more on Trust and limits.

Who can see my clips?

Only you, on your box. A receipt shows fingerprints, times and a camera key. It shows no picture and no sound.

How long are my clips kept?

The box deletes old clips after 14 days by default. Receipts stay. Download any clip you may need as evidence before it is deleted. You can change the setting.

What happens when the pilot ends?

The test network can be reset after 15 Dec 2026. A reset can remove pilot records.

When the main network opens, you would need to seal again. We plan to tell you before that happens and to guide you. We collect nothing from you until then.
