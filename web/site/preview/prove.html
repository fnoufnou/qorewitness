Show that a clip is the original.

Two people are involved. The owner sends the clip and its receipt file. The receiver drops both on a web page and reads the result.

For the receiver

Check a video now.

You received a clip and a receipt. You need no account and no software. Everything runs in your browser. Your files stay on your computer.

  1. Open the verifier.
  2. Drop the clip and its receipt on the page.
  3. Read the result. Green means the clip has not changed since it was sealed. Red means it does not match its receipt.
  4. To keep a copy of the result, use the print function of your browser and save it as PDF.

For the owner

Get the clip and its receipt.

Do this as soon as you know you may need a clip. Clips are deleted after 14 days by default.

Coming in the pilotA one-click export as one ZIP, and a verifier that takes that ZIP, are planned. They are not built yet. Today you take two files: the clip and its receipt.

  1. Find the time

    Open the dashboard and find the date and time of the clip you need.

  2. Take the clip and the receipt

    Copy the clip from the folder ~/qorewitness/data/<camera>/segments on the box. Download its receipt from the dashboard. Keep both files together.

  3. Check it yourself

    Drop the clip and its receipt on the verifier before you send them. You should see the green result.

  4. Send it

    Send both files to the receiver, for example an insurer or a lawyer. Add the link to the verifier. You can use this text:

    Subject: Video evidence with proof of origin Hello, Attached are a video clip from my home camera and its receipt file. The footage was sealed by QoreWitness minutes after recording. To check it: 1. Open https://qorewitness.fnoufnou.com/verifier.html 2. Drop the clip and the receipt file on the page. 3. The page shows a green result if the clip has not changed since it was sealed. You do not need to trust me or QoreWitness. The page repeats every check in your own browser. Note: the video shows people. Please handle it with care.
    Your video shows peopleSend it only to people who need it. Use a way to send that you trust. Do not post it in public.

What a green result means

  • This file existed at the time the network recorded.
  • It has not changed since it was sealed.
  • The seal was made by the key of one known camera.
  • The clips around it form an unbroken recording.

What it does not mean

  • That the scene was real. A staged scene is sealed the same way.
  • That a court will accept it. A court weighs it as ordinary evidence.
  • That the clip is complete. Only sealed minutes can be checked.
