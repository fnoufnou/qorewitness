Set up your camera

Daily use

Set it up once, and it simply keeps going.

Keep using your camera app for live view and alerts, just as you do today. QoreWitness works quietly in the background, and your dashboard tells you at a glance that all is well.

Your dashboard

It shows one status line in a colour, and when something needs you, exactly one thing to do. Bookmark it on your phone and laptop. On a Raspberry Pi named qorewitness, the address is usually http://qorewitness.local:8080.

ColourIt saysWhat to do
GreenSealing OK.Nothing at all.
OrangeWaiting for the first seal.Nothing. It arrives shortly after setup.
OrangeThe disk is almost full.Keep video for fewer days, or connect a bigger disk.
RedNo video from the camera.Check the camera's power and Wi-Fi.
RedSealing stopped.Check that your QoreWitness computer is online, and restart it if the message stays.
Using your own wallet: fee messages

If you chose Use my own wallet in the setup, you pay the network fees yourself. When the dashboard says the fees are running out, add tokens to the address it shows. See Plans.

Phone alerts are on our roadmap. For now, you see the status whenever you open the dashboard.

Bookmark it

Add the dashboard to your phone's home screen and it opens like an app. Every row is a sealed part of your recording, with its own Download button for the day you need it.

About two weeks of video, and seals for good

QoreWitness keeps about two weeks of recordings, depending on your disk, and clears the oldest to make room. The seals stay. A clip that has been cleared cannot be proven, so press Save evidence for anything you may need later. Prove a video shows how.

Power cuts and restarts

  • Your QoreWitness computer restarts. Sealing picks up again by itself.
  • The camera loses power. Nothing is recorded while it is off, and every earlier seal stays valid. The dashboard turns red until the camera is back.
  • The internet goes down. Recording carries on. Footage from while the connection was down may have no seal, and the public record shows that gap openly.

Pause or remove

  • To pause, switch off your QoreWitness computer. Your camera and its app keep working as usual.
  • To remove QoreWitness, see Developers.
  • Seals on the public record cannot be deleted. They hold only fingerprints and times.
Advanced: back up your QoreWitness computer

If the computer breaks, you set QoreWitness up again with a new camera seal, and every earlier seal stays checkable. To keep the same camera identity, copy the folder ~/qorewitness/data to a USB stick and keep it safe. Anyone with these files can use your account, which is why QoreWitness never asks for them.

Keep QoreWitness on your home networkUse a network you trust, and never open port 8080 to the internet in your router.

Need to prove a clip?

Press Save evidence, share the file, and let anyone check it with QoreWitness Verify.
