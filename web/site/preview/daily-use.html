Daily use

Nothing changes in your day.

Keep using the Tapo app for live view and alerts. QoreWitness works in the background, on your box. Open the dashboard once in a while.

The dashboard

The dashboard shows one line in a colour and one thing to do. Open it at the address of your box, for example http://192.168.1.30:8080, and sign in with your dashboard password.

ColourExampleWhat to do
GreenSealing OK. Last seal 4 minutes ago.Nothing.
OrangeWaiting for the first seal.Nothing. It comes 10 to 15 minutes after setup.
OrangeNetwork fees low: about 2 days left.Self-managed: add test QOR to the address shown. Fees included: the pilot tops up.
OrangeThe disk is almost full.Keep video for fewer days, or connect a bigger disk.
RedNo video from the camera.Check the power and Wi-Fi of the camera. Check its IP address.
RedSealing stopped 40 minutes ago.Check the internet connection of the box. Restart the box if this stays.
RedSealing stops: the network fees ran out.Self-managed: add test QOR. Fees included: the pilot tops up. If this stays for a day, the pilot may be paused.

Phone alerts for these states are planned. Today you see them when you open the dashboard.

Clips are kept for 14 days

The box deletes video older than the retention time. The setup proposes it from the free disk. The default is 14 days. Receipts stay. A clip that is gone cannot be proven, so download a clip before it is deleted if you may need it. See Prove a video.

Power cuts and restarts

  • Box restarts. Sealing starts again by itself. You do nothing.
  • Camera loses power. No video is recorded while it is off. Earlier seals stay valid. The dashboard turns red until the camera is back.
  • Internet is down. Recording goes on. The minutes without a connection probably have no seal. The network shows the gap with its start and length.
  • Crash during sealing. After a restart, the box rebuilds the lost receipt from public network data. We tested this.

Backup

Copy these things to a USB stick or another computer. Keep them away from the box.

  • The folder qorewitness/data, at least the file qorewitness.env and the folder keys. These are your keys. Anyone with them can use your test account.
  • The clips you may need, with their receipts. A one-click export as one ZIP is coming in the pilot. Until then, copy the clip by hand and download its receipt from the dashboard.

The seals on the network stay if you lose the box. To prove a clip you also need the clip and its receipt.

Update, stop or remove

  • Update. QoreWitness never updates itself. The version is pinned on purpose. You choose when to move to a new version.
  • Pause or remove. Stop QoreWitness with cd ~/qorewitness && docker compose down. Your camera and the Tapo app keep working. Delete the folder ~/qorewitness when you no longer need the video, the receipts or the keys.
  • Records on the network cannot be deleted by us or by you. They hold only fingerprints and times.
  • If you stop using a camera key, you can revoke it. A revoked key cannot come back.
Keep the box on your home networkThe setup and the dashboard use plain HTTP on your home network. Use a network you trust. Never forward port 8080 on your router.

Need proof of a clip?

Take the clip and its receipt, send them, and let the other side check them in a browser.
