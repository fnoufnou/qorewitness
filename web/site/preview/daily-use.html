Daily use

Nothing changes in your day.

Keep using the Tapo app for live view and alerts. QoreWitness works in the background. Open the dashboard once in a while.

The dashboard

It shows one line in a colour and one thing to do. Bookmark its address on your phone and laptop. On a Pi named qorewitness it often is http://qorewitness.local:8080.

ColourIt saysWhat to do
GreenSealing OK. Last seal 4 minutes ago.Nothing.
OrangeWaiting for the first seal.Nothing. It comes 10 to 15 minutes after setup.
OrangeNetwork fees low.Nothing. The pilot tops up.
OrangeThe disk is almost full.Keep video for fewer days, or connect a bigger disk.
RedNo video from the camera.Check the power and Wi-Fi of the camera.
RedSealing stopped 40 minutes ago.Check the internet of the box. Restart the box if this stays.
Self-managed plan: fee messages

With Self-managed you add free test tokens yourself. If the line says the network fees are low or ran out, add test QOR to the address shown. See Plans.

Phone alerts are planned. Today you see these states when you open the dashboard.

Clips are kept for about 2 weeks

The box deletes old video after about 2 weeks. Receipts stay. A clip that is gone cannot be proven. Press Save evidence for any clip you may need. See Prove a video.

Power cuts and restarts

  • The box restarts. Sealing starts again by itself.
  • The camera loses power. No video is recorded while it is off. Earlier seals stay valid. The dashboard turns red until the camera is back.
  • The internet is down. Recording goes on. The minutes without a connection probably have no seal. The public record shows the gap.

Pause or remove

  • To pause, unplug the box. Your camera and the Tapo app keep working.
  • To remove QoreWitness, see For developers.
  • Records on the public record cannot be deleted. They hold only fingerprints and times.
Advanced: back up the box

If the box breaks, you set it up again with a new camera. Seals made before stay checkable. To keep the same camera identity, copy the folder ~/qorewitness/data to a USB stick. Anyone with these files can use your test account. QoreWitness never asks for them.

Keep the box on your home networkUse a network you trust. Never forward port 8080 on your router.

Need proof of a clip?

Press Save evidence, send the file, and let the other side check it in a browser.
