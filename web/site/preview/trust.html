Trust and limits

What it proves, and what it cannot.

A proof is only as good as what goes into it, so here is the plain list. We publish what our own tests catch and what they miss.

What it proves

  • The file existed at the time of the ledger record. It is sealed within one 10-minute window of recording.
  • It has not changed since it was sealed.
  • The seal was made with the key of one known camera.
  • The clip sits in an unbroken recording. A removed, inserted or reordered clip shows, and a gap in recording shows with its start and length.
  • The time is bounded on both sides: the seal was made after a recent block and recorded before a later block.

What it cannot prove

  • That the scene was real. A staged scene or a fake stream is sealed in the same way.
  • Who recorded it. It shows which camera key signed.
  • That nobody fed the QoreWitness computer fake footage before sealing. That computer is the trust boundary.
  • That a court accepts it. We are not an EU qualified time stamp provider, and a court weighs a seal like other evidence.

The beta

What the beta on the test network means for you

QoreWitness runs on qorechain-diana, the QoreChain test network. The post-quantum checks it relies on are live there today and not yet on the QoreChain main network.

  • The test network can be reset. The QoreChain team says it will not reset before 15 Dec 2026. After that, a reset can remove every seal made during the beta.
  • Seals from the beta show how the method works. They are not meant as evidence yet, because the test network can be reset. Keep the original files.
  • Fees included depends on us. The service operator (fnoufnou) pays the network fees with test tokens that have no value. The service can stop, and you can then switch to Self-managed.
  • No payment is collected during the beta. When the main network opens, seals would need to be made again there. We plan to tell you before that happens and to guide you through it.

Threat model, summary

Who could attack it, and what happens.

We ran these drills on the live system ourselves. No outside firm ran them. The full texts are in the threat model and the custody drill.

AttackResultWhy
Change one byte of a sealed clipDetectedThe file fingerprint is no longer in the seal file.
Remove or reorder a clip in the seal fileDetectedThe chain of clips breaks.
Seal a second version of an old windowRefusedThe contract refuses overlapping windows.
Swap in footage from another camera, re-encode, delete or add a clip before sealingFlaggedThe record shows a stream change, a gap or an overlap. In our drill, 4 of 5 such attacks were flagged.
Swap in footage that uses the camera's own settings, before sealingMissedThe QoreWitness computer cannot tell. This is the trust boundary.
Steal the QoreWitness computerRiskIt holds your camera key and, today, the keys of your account on the test network. Keep it safe. A mode that keeps the main key offline is planned.
An attacker with only the classic (ECDSA) key of the accountFailedIn a dry run of the Q-Day Drill, 13 attacks failed and 0 succeeded. Every change needs a fresh post-quantum signature.

How it was reviewed

Privacy

What leaves your home.

This is a summary and not legal advice. It reflects the GDPR (AVG) as we understand it.

PartDataOur rule
Your QoreWitness computerVideo of people at and near your home. The camera address and camera password.Video stays on your computer, and we never receive it. QoreWitness sends no usage data to us.
Public ledgerAccount address, camera id, times and fingerprints.A fixed rhythm. Fingerprints show no content. We use no names or addresses as labels.
Public ledger nodeThe node that QoreWitness talks to can see your IP address.A known limit.
This websiteYour IP address in server logs.No trackers, and no email addresses collected.
  • Records on a public ledger cannot be deleted. Keep names and addresses out of camera labels.
  • A fingerprint of a video with people in it may count as personal data in some cases. Everything else stays off the chain.
If your camera films the street or a neighbourThe household exemption likely does not apply then (CJEU, case C-212/13, Rynes). You are the controller of that video. Aim the camera at your own property, put up a sign, and read the guidance on home cameras from your data protection authority. In the Netherlands this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

QoreWitness will never ask for your passwords or files.
