Get started

Set up your Tapo C210.

Seven steps from camera to your first seal. You run one command. Everything else is in the Tapo app and in your browser.

  • About 1 hour
  • Free during the pilot
  • One command in a terminal
Guide statusWe write this guide before the first test on a real C210 on 24 Nov 2026. Pictures are marked "Screenshot" and come after that test. Some steps may change. Last checked on a real camera: not yet.

Steps

  1. Make a camera account
  2. Find the camera address
  3. Check the recording options
  4. Install on the box
  5. Open the setup wizard
  6. Choose a plan
  7. Your first seal

What you need

  • A TP-Link Tapo C210, powered, on your Wi-Fi, and set up in the Tapo app.
  • A small Linux box that stays on. Our reference device is a Raspberry Pi 5 (4 GB) with Raspberry Pi OS Lite 64-bit and a USB SSD. Any Debian or Ubuntu machine with Docker also works. We confirm the exact shopping list after the first test.
  • Room for video. Plan for several GB per day. A 128 GB card or an SSD is a safe start.
  • A phone or laptop on the same Wi-Fi as the box.
Aim the camera at your own propertyIf your camera films the street or a neighbour, privacy law may apply to you. Put up a sign and read the guidance of your data protection authority. In the Netherlands this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens. See Trust and limits.

  1. Make a camera account

    About 3 minutes · Tapo app

    The box reads the video stream with a separate camera account. It is not your Tapo login. You make it in the Tapo app.

    1. Open the Tapo app and tap your camera.
    2. Tap the gear icon (Settings).
    3. Tap Advanced Settings.
    4. Tap Camera Account.
    5. Agree to the notice.
    6. Choose a user name and a password. Each has 6 to 32 characters.
    Keep the password plainUse letters and digits. Special characters can make the stream fail. Write the user name and password down. You need them in step 5.
    Something wrong?
    • You cannot find Camera Account. Update the Tapo app and the camera firmware, then look again.
    • The app says the password is not allowed. Use only letters and digits, 6 to 32 characters.

  2. Find the camera address

    About 2 minutes · Tapo app

    Your camera has an address on your home network, for example 192.168.1.50. The wizard asks for it.

    1. In the Tapo app, tap your camera, then the gear icon.
    2. Open Device Info.
    3. Write down the IP Address.

    The address can change after a router restart. In your router, give the camera a fixed address ("address reservation"). This keeps the stream working.

    Something wrong?
    • The IP address field is empty. Check that the camera is online in the app.
    • You do not know how to reserve the address. Search for your router model and "DHCP reservation". It takes a few minutes.

  3. Check the recording options

    About 2 minutes · Tapo app

    TP-Link says a Tapo camera can run only two of these three at the same time: Tapo Care, microSD recording and NVR recording. Your box reads the stream like a recorder. If the test in step 5 says the stream is busy or off, switch one of the three off and test again.

    We confirm this rule for QoreWitness on the first real test. We update this step after that.

  4. Install on the box

    About 10 minutes · Terminal on the box

    Open a terminal on the box. Use SSH from your laptop or a keyboard and screen. Paste this one command and press Enter.

    curl -fsSL https://qorewitness.fnoufnou.com/install.sh | sh

    The command installs what the box needs, downloads QoreWitness and starts it. At the end, it prints a link and a code in large text. Keep the window open until you have opened the link.

    Want to read the script first?Open https://qorewitness.fnoufnou.com/install.sh in your browser. It is plain text. The code is open source on GitHub.
    Something wrong?
    • curl: command not found. Install it with sudo apt install curl and run the command again.
    • The command stops with an error. Run it again. It is safe to run twice. If it fails again, ask on X or the QoreChain Discord and paste the last lines.
    • You lost the link. Run the command again. It prints the link again.

  5. Open the setup wizard

    About 15 minutes · Browser

    On your phone or laptop, on the same Wi-Fi, open the link from step 4 and type the code. The wizard guides you in small screens.

    1. Choose a password for your dashboard. Use at least 10 characters.
    2. Type the camera address from step 2, and the user name and password from step 1. Give the camera a name, for example "Front door".
    3. Tick the box that says the camera films your own property.
    4. Press Test camera. You should see a picture from your camera within 15 seconds.
    5. Press Looks right. The box now makes a key for this camera. You do not type anything for this.
    Something wrong?
    • The camera refused the password. Check the camera account in the Tapo app (step 1). Check upper and lower case.
    • Camera not found. Check the address (step 2). Check that the box and the camera are on the same network.
    • Stream busy or off. Look at step 3.
    • No picture, but no error. Press Use standard quality. The camera then sends the lighter stream.
    • You closed the browser by mistake. Open the link again. The wizard continues where it stopped.

  6. Choose a plan

    About 10 minutes · Browser

    Both plans are free during the testnet pilot. The difference is who looks after the network fees. See Plans for the full picture.

    PlanWhat you do
    Self-managedThe wizard makes your own account and shows a recovery phrase of 24 words. Write it on paper and keep it safe. Then you get free test tokens from the faucet. The wizard waits until the account has enough.
    Fees includedYou enter a pilot code. We pay the network fees for your camera. Pilot places are limited. Ask for a pilot code.

    Our advice: pick Self-managed unless you hold a pilot code.

    The recovery phrase is yours aloneNobody can recover it for you. QoreWitness will never ask for it. Not on this site and not in a message. Anyone who asks is not us.
    Something wrong?
    • The faucet says you reached the limit. Wait 24 hours and try again.
    • The wizard says no pilot places are left. Choose Self-managed.

  7. Your first seal

    About 15 minutes · Browser

    The wizard registers your camera on the network. A progress list shows each part. When it is done you see Sealing started.

    The first seal appears in about 10 to 15 minutes. Your dashboard then shows a green line: Sealing OK, last seal a few minutes ago.

    Done. What now?Your Tapo app works as before. QoreWitness runs in the background. Read Daily use. When you need proof of a clip, read Prove a video.
    Something wrong?
    • No green line after 30 minutes. Open the dashboard and read the line at the top. It says in plain words what is missing.
    • The line is red and says there is no video from the camera. Check the power and the Wi-Fi of the camera.
    • Still stuck. Ask on X or the QoreChain Discord.
