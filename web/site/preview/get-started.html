Get started

Set up your Tapo C210.

Five steps. You paste one line on your own computer. The rest is the Tapo app and your browser.

  • About 1 hour
  • Free during the pilot
Guide statusWe test this guide on a real C210 on 24 Nov 2026. Pictures come after that test. Last checked on a real camera: not yet.

Steps

  1. Make a camera account
  2. Find the camera address
  3. Prepare the box
  4. Paste one line
  5. Open the link

What you need

  • A TP-Link Tapo C210, on your Wi-Fi, set up in the Tapo app.
  • A box that stays on: a Raspberry Pi 5 with a USB SSD, or any always-on Linux computer. Use 128 GB or more.
  • The camera, the box and your computer on the same home network.
Aim the camera at your own propertyIf it films the street or a neighbour, privacy law may apply to you. Put up a sign. See Trust and limits.

  1. Make a camera account

    3 minutes · Tapo app

    1. Open the Tapo app and tap your camera.
    2. Tap the gear icon (Settings).
    3. Tap Advanced Settings, then Camera Account.
    4. Agree to the notice.
    5. Choose a user name and a password. Each has 6 to 32 characters.

    Use letters and digits only. Special characters can make the video fail. Write both down.

  2. Find the camera address

    2 minutes · Tapo app

    In the Tapo app, tap your camera, then the gear icon, then Device Info. Write down the IP address, for example 192.168.1.20.

    Tip: keep the address fixed

    If your router gives the camera a new address, the box loses it. In your router, reserve the address for the camera. Search for your router model and "DHCP reservation".

  3. Prepare the box

    15 minutes · Your computer

    For a Raspberry Pi 5:

    1. Install Raspberry Pi Imager and choose Raspberry Pi OS Lite (64-bit).
    2. In its settings, set the host name to qorewitness, choose a user name and password, enter your Wi-Fi (or use a cable) and turn on SSH.
    3. Write the card, put it in the Pi and start it. Wait two minutes.
    Another Linux computer

    Use any 64-bit Debian or Ubuntu computer with internet access and a user with sudo. In step 4, paste only the part in quotes into its own terminal.

  4. Paste one line

    10 minutes · Terminal on your computer

    Open a terminal on your own computer. On a Mac, open Terminal. On Windows, open Terminal or PowerShell.

    Paste this line. Change USER to the user name you chose in step 3. Press Enter.

    ssh -t USER@qorewitness.local "curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fnoufnou/qorewitness/v0.6.0-alpha.1/install.sh | bash -s -- --yes"

    The terminal asks once if you trust the box. Type yes. Then it asks for the password you chose in step 3. The installer does the rest. At the end it shows a link and a code.

    Something wrong?
    • qorewitness.local not found. Use the IP address of the box from your router app instead of qorewitness.local.
    • Permission denied. Check the user name and password you chose in step 3.
    • Do not share the code. Anyone with it can set up the box until you choose a password.
    • Want to read the script first? Open it on GitHub. You can run the line again at any time.

  5. Open the link

    15 minutes · Browser

    Open the link on your computer or phone and type the code. Three screens follow. Then a progress bar.

    1. Password. Choose a password for the dashboard.
    2. Camera. Type the camera address from step 2 and the user name and password from step 1. Tick that the camera films your own property. Press Test camera.
    3. Is this your camera? You see a picture from your camera within 15 seconds. Press Looks right.

    A progress bar connects your camera. It takes a few minutes. You can close the page. Then you see Sealing started. The pilot pays the network fees. You do nothing.

    The first seal comes in about 10 to 15 minutes. Open the dashboard. The line turns green when sealing works. Save the dashboard address as a bookmark.

    Done.Keep using the Tapo app for live view and alerts. Read Daily use. To prove a clip, read Prove a video.
    Something wrong?
    • The camera refused the password. Make the camera account again with letters and digits only.
    • No camera answers. Check the address in Device Info. Check that the camera is on.
    • The camera is busy, or sends no picture. Close the live view in the Tapo app. Tapo Care, the microSD card and a recorder such as QoreWitness: only two of the three work at the same time. Turn one off.
    • The pilot is full. The page says so and offers the other plan. See Plans.
    • You forgot the password. Run the line in step 4 again with --new-password added after --yes.
