Get started

Set up your Tapo C210.

Seven steps from a new camera to Sealing started. You type one command in a terminal. Everything else is in the Tapo app and in your browser.

  • About 1 hour
  • Free during the pilot
  • One command in a terminal
Guide statusThe installer, the setup in the browser and the dashboard are built. We test the whole guide on a real C210 on 24 Nov 2026. Pictures are marked "Screenshot" and come after that test. Last checked on a real camera: not yet.

Steps

  1. Make a camera account
  2. Find the camera address
  3. Check the recording options
  4. Prepare the box
  5. Run the installer
  6. Set up in the browser
  7. Your first seal

What you need

  • A TP-Link Tapo C210, powered, on your Wi-Fi, and set up in the Tapo app.
  • A box that stays on: a Raspberry Pi 5 with a USB SSD, or any always-on Linux computer (64-bit Debian or Ubuntu with internet access). When the box is off, it records and seals nothing.
  • Room for video: 128 GB or more. A USB SSD lasts longer than an SD card. High quality video likely needs 15 to 20 GB a day (we still measure this).
  • The camera and the box on the same home network, and a phone or laptop on that network.
Aim the camera at your own propertyIf your camera films the street or a neighbour, privacy law may apply to you. Put up a sign and read the guidance of your data protection authority. In the Netherlands this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens. See Trust and limits.

  1. Make a camera account

    About 3 minutes · Tapo app

    The box reads the video from the camera over your home network. The camera needs its own account for this. It is not your TP-Link login.

    1. Open the Tapo app and tap your camera.
    2. Tap the gear icon (Settings).
    3. Tap Advanced Settings.
    4. Tap Camera Account.
    5. Agree to the notice.
    6. Choose a user name and a password. Each has 6 to 32 characters.
    Keep the password plainUse letters and digits. Special characters can make the stream fail. Use a new password. Do not reuse the password of your TP-Link account. Write the user name and password down. You need them in step 6.
    Something wrong?
    • You cannot find Camera Account. Update the Tapo app and the camera firmware, then look again.
    • The app says the password is not allowed. Use only letters and digits, 6 to 32 characters.

  2. Find the camera address

    About 2 minutes · Tapo app

    Your camera has an address on your home network, for example 192.168.1.20. The setup asks for it.

    1. In the Tapo app, tap your camera, then the gear icon.
    2. Open Device Info.
    3. Write down the IP address.

    The address can change after a router restart, and then the box loses the camera. In your router, give the camera a fixed address. This is often called "DHCP reservation".

    Something wrong?
    • The IP address field is empty. Check that the camera is online in the app.
    • You do not know how to reserve the address. Search for your router model and "DHCP reservation". It takes a few minutes.

  3. Check the recording options

    About 2 minutes · Tapo app

    Tapo Care (cloud), the microSD card and a recorder such as QoreWitness: only two of the three work at the same time. If all three are on, turn one off. The C210 also sends at most two streams at once, and the live view in the Tapo app counts as one.

  4. Prepare the box

    About 15 minutes · Your computer

    Raspberry Pi 5:

    1. Install Raspberry Pi Imager on your computer.
    2. Choose Raspberry Pi OS Lite (64-bit).
    3. In the settings of the Imager: host name qorewitness, a user name and password, your Wi-Fi (or use a cable) and Enable SSH.
    4. Write the card, put it in the Pi and start it. Wait two minutes.
    5. On your computer, open a terminal and connect: ssh <your user>@qorewitness.local

    Another Linux computer: any 64-bit Debian or Ubuntu install with internet access and a user with sudo. A mini PC or a virtual machine works.

    A ready-made Pi image is planned. Today you install Raspberry Pi OS yourself.

  5. Run the installer

    About 10 minutes · Terminal on the box

    In the terminal of the box, paste this one line and press Enter.

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fnoufnou/qorewitness/main/install.sh | bash

    The installer checks the system, offers to install Docker if it is missing (it asks first), downloads QoreWitness and starts it. At the end it shows a setup link and a pairing code of 6 digits, in large text.

    Do not share the codeAnyone with the code can set up the box until you choose a password. The link and the code are also in the file ~/qorewitness/SETUP-LINK.txt. Only your user can read it.
    Want to read the script first?Open it on GitHub. The code is open source. You can run the installer again at any time. It keeps your camera, keys, account and video.
    Something wrong?
    • curl: command not found. Install it with sudo apt install curl and run the line again.
    • You lost the link or the code. Run the installer again. It shows them again.
    • The page does not open later. The box and your phone must be on the same network. Guest Wi-Fi often blocks this.
    • Still stuck. Ask on X or in the QoreChain Discord and paste the last lines.

  6. Set up in the browser

    About 15 minutes · Phone or laptop

    Open the setup link on your phone or laptop and type the pairing code. The setup moves in small screens. You can close the browser at any time. Open the link again and the setup continues at the right step.

    1. Password. Choose a password for the dashboard. Use 10 characters or more.
    2. Camera. Type the camera address (step 2) and the user name and password of the camera account (step 1). Give the camera a name, for example camera-1. Tick the box that the camera films your own property. Press Test camera. Within 15 seconds you see a picture from your camera. Press Looks right. For a smaller disk or weak Wi-Fi, press Use standard quality.
    3. Plan. Choose who pays the network fees. See the table below.
    4. Keys. Press Create keys. The box makes an account key and a camera key. Read the backup note, tick the box and continue.
    5. Network fees. Fees included: the pilot adds the fees and the page moves on by itself. Self-managed: copy the address, claim free test tokens in the Faucet of the QoreChain dashboard and wait. You need at least 2 test QOR.
    6. Connect. Press Start. A progress bar shows four steps. It takes 1 to 3 minutes.
    7. Done. The page says Sealing started.
    PlanWhat you do
    Fees included (pilot)You enter a pilot code. The pilot service adds free test tokens to your own account. Pilot places are limited. Ask for a pilot code.
    Self-managedYou hold the account and add free test tokens yourself, about once a week.

    Both plans make the same proof. Both are free in the pilot. Our advice: choose Self-managed unless you hold a pilot code.

    Back up your keysYour keys are files on the box, in the folder qorewitness/data. Copy that folder to a USB stick and keep it somewhere safe. If the box breaks, these files are the only way to keep this camera and its history. Anyone with these files can use your test account. QoreWitness never asks for them, by email or on a website.
    Something wrong?
    • The camera refused the password. Make the camera account again in the Tapo app, with letters and digits only.
    • No camera answers at this address. Check the address in Device Info. Check that the camera is on and in Wi-Fi range.
    • The camera is busy. Close the live view in the Tapo app and test again.
    • The camera sends no picture. Look at step 3. Turn one of the three recording options off.
    • The pilot says the code is not valid, used or the pilot is full. Check the code, or choose Self-managed.
    • You forgot the dashboard password. Run the installer again with the option --new-password. Camera, keys and account stay.

  7. Your first seal

    About 15 minutes · Browser

    The first seal comes in about 10 to 15 minutes. Press Open my dashboard. The dashboard shows one line in a colour and one thing to do. At first it says Waiting for the first seal in orange. This is normal. Then it turns green: Sealing OK. Last seal 4 minutes ago.

    Done. What now?Keep using the Tapo app for live view and alerts. Nothing changes there. Read Daily use. When you need proof of a clip, read Prove a video.
    Something wrong?
    • No green line after 30 minutes. Read the line on the dashboard. It says what to do.
    • The line is red and says there is no video from the camera. Check the power and Wi-Fi of the camera and its IP address.
    • Still stuck. Ask on X or in the QoreChain Discord.
