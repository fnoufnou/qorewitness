Get started
Self-managed: for people with their own wallet

Free software. Always. You hold your own account and pay the network fees yourself, with your own QoreChain wallet.

  • During the pilot, you get free test tokens from the faucet. About 0.1 test token per seal.
  • Later, you buy tokens for the fees.
  • If you lose your keys and have no backup, nobody can recover them for you.

You choose this plan in the setup when you want it. It makes the same proof.

Plans and prices can change before the main network opens. We will ask before we charge anything. If we stop the pilot, you can switch to Self-managed.
