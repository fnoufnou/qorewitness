How it works

Your camera, now a witness.

As your camera records, QoreWitness quietly builds a chain of digital fingerprints and pins them to a public ledger. Here is what happens, in plain words.

Your video stays at home.

Your recordings never leave your own computer. QoreWitness creates a digital fingerprint of your video stream and keeps that, and only that, on a public record, together with the time and a seal. A fingerprint cannot be turned back into a picture or a sound.

Think of a sealed letterYou press a wax seal on a letter, keep the letter somewhere safe, and the postmark shows when it was sent. Anyone can inspect the seal and the postmark. QoreWitness does the same for video, with a digital seal and a public ledger as the postmark.

Four steps

Fingerprint, bundle, seal, record.

All of this runs by itself in the background, for as long as your camera records.

  1. 01

    Fingerprint

    QoreWitness cuts the video stream into short clips and gives each one a unique digital fingerprint. Change a single pixel and the fingerprint changes completely.

  2. 02

    Bundle

    At regular intervals it bundles the fingerprints into one compact summary, and links each clip to the one before it, so no clip can be removed or slipped in unnoticed.

  3. 03

    Seal

    Each camera gets its own digital seal, built to withstand the quantum computers of the future, and signs every summary with it.

  4. 04

    Record

    The public ledger checks the seal before it accepts the summary and its time. A small receipt stays with your recordings at home.

The picture

What goes where.

  1. Stays at home

    Your camera

    Any camera with an RTSP or ONVIF stream.

  2. Stays at home

    Your computer

    Records the clips, creates the seals and keeps both safe.

  3. Public ledger

    Checks each seal and stores the summary with its time. It holds no video.

  4. QoreWitness Verify

    Takes your evidence file and repeats every check in the browser.

What it cannot do

  • It cannot prove that the scene was real. A staged scene is sealed in exactly the same way.
  • It cannot see what happens to the video before it is sealed, so keep your camera and your QoreWitness computer safe.
  • It is not an EU qualified time stamp, and a court decides for itself how much weight to give it.

Read Trust and limits

Technical namesThe full technical detail is on the Developers page.
  • Fingerprints: SHA-256 and SHA3-256, two independent hash functions.
  • The summary: a Merkle root of the fingerprints in one time window.
  • The seal: an ML-DSA-87 signature (NIST FIPS 204) made with the camera key. Common signatures such as ECDSA and RSA may break once large quantum computers exist.
  • The public ledger: the AnchorRegistry smart contract on QoreChain. It checks the signature on chain before it stores the summary.
