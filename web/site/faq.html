FAQ

Questions people ask.

Clear answers to the questions we hear most. If yours is not here, we are happy to help on X or in the QoreChain Discord.

Does my video leave my home?

No. Your recordings stay on your own computer at home. QoreWitness creates a digital fingerprint of your video stream and keeps that, and only that, on a public record. A fingerprint reveals nothing about what is in the picture or the sound.

Which cameras work?

Any camera that can share its video stream on your home network over RTSP or ONVIF, which many wired home cameras can. Battery cameras and cameras that only stream to their maker's cloud cannot, because they never offer that stream. See the camera list, and learn how to check your own camera in two minutes.

We have no partnership with any camera maker.

What does QoreWitness run on?

On an always-on computer at home: a Raspberry Pi 4 or 5, a mini PC, a home server or a NAS. It runs anywhere Docker runs. See What you need for the minimum and recommended hardware.

Do I need to know anything about crypto?

Not at all. QoreWitness uses a public ledger as an independent notary, and that is the only part it plays. You never buy, hold or trade anything: during the beta we cover the network fees for you, and after launch the Hands-off plan does the same.

What does it cost?

Nothing during the beta. QoreWitness is free, and we cover the network fees on the test network, so you need no wallet at all.

When QoreWitness launches on the QoreChain main network, the Free plan has no subscription, and the small network fees come from your own wallet. If you would rather not think about fees, you can choose Hands-off for USD 10 or EUR 9 per camera per month. See Plans, and rest assured that we will never charge you without asking first.

What if my internet goes down?

Your camera and QoreWitness keep recording. Footage from while the connection was down may have no seal, and the public record shows that gap openly, with its start and its length.

Nobody can fill the gap later with a backdated seal.

Will the police or a court accept it?

A seal gives your footage strong, independent support that anyone can check. A court decides for itself how much weight to give it, just as with other evidence, and QoreWitness is not an EU qualified time stamp service. For a specific case, ask a lawyer.

What if my QoreWitness computer breaks?

Your seals live on the public ledger, independent of your computer. During the beta, seals live on the QoreChain test network, which can be reset. What the beta means for you. To prove a clip you also need the clip itself, so press Save evidence in the dashboard for anything that matters to you and keep that file somewhere safe.

Can QoreWitness show that the scene was real?

No. It proves that the file has not changed since it was sealed. A staged scene or a fake stream would be sealed in exactly the same way.

Can someone fake a clip from inside my home network?

It is hard, but possible for someone who already controls your QoreWitness computer or your camera, because they could feed it fake footage before it is sealed.

Keep the computer and the camera on a network you trust, and use strong passwords. There is more on Trust and limits.

Does QoreWitness record sound?

Yes, whenever your camera's microphone is switched on, QoreWitness keeps the sound together with the picture and seals both in the same fingerprint, so a clip with sound is just as easy to prove. Would you rather keep only the picture? Simply switch the microphone off in your camera's app and QoreWitness follows your choice.

Sound travels further than you might expect, so please point your camera at your own home and garden, let visitors know with a small sign that you record picture and sound, and read the advice of your national data protection authority on cameras at home (in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Who can see my recordings?

Only you, on your own computer. A receipt shows fingerprints and times, and nothing of the picture or the sound.

How long are my recordings kept?

QoreWitness keeps about two weeks of video, depending on your disk, and your seals live on the public ledger, independent of your computer. During the beta, seals live on the QoreChain test network, which can be reset. What the beta means for you. Press Save evidence for any clip you may need later, before it is cleared to make room.
